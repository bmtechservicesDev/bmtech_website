# Brand and product experience enhancement

## Sources and interpretation

The attached one-page brief and recovered Website UX Brief decisions establish three offerings: own products, custom development, complete digital transformation. Product areas: Restaurant Solution, Clinic Automation, My School, IoT Gateway. Navigation: Products, Industries, Solutions, Resources, demo CTA. Existing profile services and verified contact channels are retained. Full verbatim thread was not exposed by context retrieval; recovered decisions and attached blueprint drove implementation.

The supplied approved BMTech artwork carries “Engineering Intelligence | Transforming Business” and “AI • Cloud • IoT • Automation • Digital Transformation”. It is used unchanged in optimized formats. The business name remains BM Tech Services.

## Design decisions

Navy masthead and hero integrate the supplied dark logo. Cyan/blue convey the engineering identity; orange is restrained to illustration detail. Spacious white/soft-blue sections distinguish offerings. CSS product illustrations are illustrative product-area symbols, not application screenshots or feature evidence. Semantic cards lead to preselected demo/project enquiries. Resources are original planning prompts, not fabricated articles or downloads.

Eight static routes retain the framework and existing service capability page. Responsive cards collapse from four to two to one column. Header navigation retains Escape/outside dismissal and focus return. Contact retains persistent validation, announced draft state, clipboard fallback and honest visitor-sent email/WhatsApp actions.

## Remaining configuration

Product specifications, commercial availability, pricing, approved domain, privacy policy and any social links require owner-approved details. A live AI service and server-side lead delivery are not configured. No merge or production deployment is performed by this change.

## Verification

Initial implementation passed all 16 CI tests. Screenshot review covered all eight desktop pages plus phone/tablet layouts. Review identified hero-to-illustration spacing on small screens; grid spacing was corrected. Mobile Contact puts the form before the lengthy contact details. Final CI reruns validate these refinements. There is no lint/type-check script in the JavaScript repository; syntax, build and meaningful browser tests are the available checks.
